⚠️ Out-of-Warranty ICE Repair Risk & Emergency Reserve Budgeting Financial Vulnerability

Historical Precedent (98,000 km Milestone): The Almera N18 experienced a sudden unexpected mechanical repair of RM 3,820.00. When combined with periodic maintenance, the single workshop bill skyrocketed to RM 4,393.00.

  • Personal Savings Impact: Unplanned spikes of ~RM 4,000 directly force you to "korek" personal savings or emergency funds if cash buffers aren't prepared in advance.
  • True Cost to Retain Almera: To safely keep the Almera past 100,000 km without risking emergency cash reserves, a minimum buffer of RM 150.00 – RM 200.00/month must be set aside strictly for unexpected wear-and-tear failures.
  • Effective Adjusted Monthly Running Cost: Adding this emergency repair buffer raises the Almera's true monthly financial commitment from RM 1,292.00 to RM 1,442.00 – RM 1,492.00.
  • EV Warranty Assurance: The Leapmotor B10 Life is backed by full manufacturer factory and high-voltage battery warranties, eliminating high-value unexpected repair bill risks during ownership.

🔧 Service & Time Efficiency Comparison

  • 66% Fewer Service Visits: The Almera requires ~3 service visits per year (~every 7,000 km), whereas the B10 Life requires only 1 annual visit (every 20,000 km).
  • Unplanned Expense Protection: Upgrading to a new EV effectively trades unpredictable repair spikes for a predictable, fixed loan payment.

  • No Battery Anxiety at 100%: Unlike NMC battery chemistry, LFP cells thrive on frequent 100% charges. It keeps the Battery Management System (BMS) accurately calibrated so range estimates stay precise.
